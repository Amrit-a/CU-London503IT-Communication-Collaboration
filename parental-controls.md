# Parental Controls

The game includes parental control features to support safe and appropriate gameplay for younger audiences.

1. Players may receive optional screen time reminders after extended gameplay sessions to encourage healthy digital habits.

2. Parents or guardians can view player progress, completed stages, scores, and awareness statistics through the progress summary screen.

3. All user accounts, progress, and game data are stored locally on the device and are not shared with external servers or third parties.

4. The game does not include any public chat systems, voice communication, friend requests, or direct messaging features between players.

5. The game does not contain microtransactions, premium currency, loot boxes, or real-money purchases of any kind.

6. Parents may adjust or disable music and sound effects through the settings menu to create a suitable gameplay environment.

7. Players are encouraged not to use their real name or any personal information when creating a username or profile.

8. Players can pause gameplay at any point to support supervised play or natural interruptions.

9. The game does not require players to provide any personal information beyond a username for local progress tracking.

10. All scenarios and educational content have been designed to be age-appropriate for players aged 12–17 and do not contain graphic, violent, or distressing material.
