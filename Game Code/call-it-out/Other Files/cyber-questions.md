# Questions

## Stage 1 — The Group Chat

**1.** You are in a class WhatsApp group. Someone posts an embarrassing photo of Jordan taken without their knowledge. People are reacting with laughing emojis. What do you do?

A) React with a laughing emoji so you do not seem different from everyone else

B) Stay silent and hope someone else says something

C) Privately message Jordan to let them know and check they are okay ✅

D) Screenshot the photo and share it in another group

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Think about what Jordan needs right now, not what looks easiest for you."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Reaching out privately to Jordan shows support without drawing more attention to the post. Jordan needs to know someone is on their side."

---

**2.** You privately message Jordan and they say they had no idea the photo was posted and feel humiliated. What should you suggest Jordan does first?

A) Send an angry message to the whole group to confront everyone

B) Report the photo to the platform and ask the sender to delete it ✅

C) Leave the group chat immediately and say nothing

D) Post a photo of the person who shared it to get back at them

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Reacting with anger often makes the situation worse. Think about what actually removes the harmful content."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Reporting the content and asking for deletion directly addresses the harm. Most platforms have clear processes for removing non-consensual photos."

---

**3.** The person who posted the photo sees that Jordan is upset and sends a message to the group saying it was just a joke. Others agree and say Jordan is overreacting. What do you do?

A) Agree that it was probably not meant to hurt anyone

B) Say nothing and leave the conversation

C) Calmly tell the group that sharing photos without someone's permission is not a joke and can cause real harm ✅

D) Tell Jordan privately they should ignore it and move on

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Staying silent when someone is being dismissed allows harmful behaviour to continue unchallenged."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Naming the behaviour calmly and clearly challenges the group's thinking without escalating the situation."

---

**4.** Jordan asks you not to tell any adults because they are embarrassed and worried it will make things worse. What do you do?

A) Agree to stay silent because it is Jordan's decision

B) Tell a trusted teacher or parent anyway because you are worried about Jordan ✅

C) Post in the group calling out everyone involved publicly

D) Tell Jordan to deal with it themselves if they will not let you help

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Respecting a friend's wishes is important, but keeping someone else's safety secret can sometimes cause more harm."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. When a friend is being harmed, involving a trusted adult is the right thing to do even if it feels difficult. A good friend protects someone even when it is uncomfortable."

## Stage 2 — The Gaming Lobby

**5.** You are playing an online game with Jordan when two classmates join the lobby and immediately start making negative comments about Jordan's gameplay. The chat is filling up with mocking messages. What do you do first?

A) Laugh along so the classmates do not turn on you

B) Leave the game and let Jordan deal with it

C) Tell the classmates in the chat to stop and that their comments are not funny ✅

D) Report the game session to your school

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Jordan can see everything happening in the chat. What message does your choice send to them?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Calling out the behaviour directly in the chat shows Jordan they are not alone and may make the others reconsider."

---

**6.** The classmates begin deliberately blocking Jordan's character in the game, preventing them from playing. Other players in the lobby are joining in and laughing. What is the best response?

A) Keep playing and hope it stops soon

B) Use the in-game report function to report the players for harassment ✅

C) Ask Jordan to create a new account to escape them

D) Start blocking Jordan's character too so the others stop targeting you

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Most online games have built-in tools specifically for this situation. Using them is not dramatic — it is exactly what they are designed for."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Reporting players through the platform's official system creates a record and may result in action being taken against the bullies."

---

**7.** After the game session Jordan messages you saying this has been happening for weeks in different games and they have started avoiding gaming altogether to escape it. What do you advise?

A) Tell Jordan to find new games where those classmates do not play

B) Suggest Jordan keeps a record of incidents including screenshots and dates, and reports it to school ✅

C) Tell Jordan to create an anonymous account and confront the bullies

D) Advise Jordan to delete all their gaming accounts

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Avoiding the bullies gives them power over Jordan's life. What would actually create a record and lead to real consequences?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Documenting incidents with screenshots and dates creates evidence that schools and platforms can act on. Avoidance alone does not resolve the problem."

## Stage 3 — The Fake Account

**8.** A social media account has been created using Jordan's name and school photo. It is posting unkind things pretending to be Jordan. Jordan sends you a screenshot and asks what to do. What is the first step?

A) Create a fake account yourself to post in defence of Jordan

B) Report the impersonation account to the social media platform immediately ✅

C) Message the account directly to find out who is behind it

D) Post on your own account warning people the account is fake

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Platforms take impersonation seriously. Think about which action actually has the power to remove the account."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Reporting impersonation to the platform is the fastest and most effective way to have the account removed. Most platforms prioritise these reports."

---

**9.** Jordan receives a message from the fake account that says: "You should just leave school. Nobody wants you there." Jordan is visibly distressed. What do you do?

A) Tell Jordan to reply and stand up for themselves

B) Tell Jordan not to read any further messages from the account and to block it, and encourage them to speak to a trusted adult about how they are feeling ✅

C) Find out who made the account and confront them directly

D) Post the message publicly to shame whoever made the account

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Jordan's wellbeing is the priority right now. What reduces harm immediately?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Blocking the account stops further direct messages. Encouraging Jordan to speak to a trusted adult means they are not dealing with the emotional impact alone."

---

**10.** You have a strong suspicion of who created the fake account based on things you have seen. What is the most appropriate action?

A) Confront the person you suspect in school and demand they delete it

B) Post on social media naming the person you suspect

C) Share your suspicion with a school staff member who can investigate appropriately ✅

D) Tell all of your mutual friends who you think did it

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Acting on suspicion without evidence can cause serious harm to an innocent person. Who has the authority to investigate this properly?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Sharing a suspicion with a trusted adult or school staff member allows a proper investigation rather than risk wrongly accusing someone."

## Stage 4 — The Pile On

**11.** A classmate directly messages you a post mocking Jordan and says: "Share this. Everyone else already has." You have not shared it. What do you do?

A) Share it because you do not want to be left out

B) Ignore the message and do not share it ✅

C) Share it but add a comment saying you feel bad about it

D) Ask the classmate why they are doing this before deciding

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Sharing harmful content makes you part of the problem regardless of your reasons. What is the simplest, most effective action here?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Not sharing the content is the single most important action. Every share extends the harm. You do not owe anyone an explanation for not participating."

---

**12.** The classmate continues pressuring you and says if you do not share it they will tell people you are friends with Jordan and start targeting you too. How do you respond?

A) Share the content to protect yourself

B) Agree to share it but never actually do so

C) Refuse to share, save the threatening message as evidence, and tell a trusted adult about the pressure you are receiving ✅

D) Threaten to report the classmate if they continue

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Being threatened for refusing to bully someone is itself a form of coercion. Who can help you in this situation?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Keeping evidence of threats and involving a trusted adult protects both you and Jordan. You should not have to manage this pressure alone."

## Stage 5 — Resolution

**13.** You want to formally report what has happened to Jordan. Which of the following is the most complete and effective approach?

A) Tell one friend and hope word gets to a teacher

B) Send an anonymous tip to the school

C) Speak directly to a trusted teacher or pastoral lead, bring screenshots and a summary of incidents, and ask what support is available for Jordan ✅

D) Post everything publicly on social media to create pressure on the school to act

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Think about what gives the school the best chance of taking meaningful action."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. A direct conversation with evidence gives the school what it needs to act. Anonymous tips are often harder to follow up. Public posts can make the situation more painful for Jordan."

---

**14.** Jordan tells you they feel like they have no friends left and do not want to go to school. What is the most important thing to do?

A) Tell Jordan things will get better and to give it time

B) Encourage Jordan to speak to a school counsellor or mental health professional, and let a trusted adult know how Jordan is feeling ✅

C) Organise a group of friends to publicly defend Jordan online

D) Suggest Jordan takes a week off school to recover

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Jordan needs professional support, not just reassurance. Who is equipped to provide that?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. A school counsellor or mental health professional is the right source of support for how Jordan is feeling. Making sure a trusted adult knows the severity is critical."

---

**15.** Some of the students involved have apologised privately to Jordan. Jordan is unsure whether to accept. What is your advice?

A) Tell Jordan they have to accept the apology to move on

B) Tell Jordan they never have to forgive anyone who hurt them

C) Encourage Jordan to take time to decide how they feel, and remind them that forgiveness is a personal choice not an obligation ✅

D) Suggest Jordan accepts publicly even if they do not mean it to avoid further conflict

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Forgiveness cannot be forced. Whose feelings should come first here?"

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Forgiveness is Jordan's choice and Jordan's timeline. Pressuring someone to forgive before they are ready can add to the harm."

**16.** Looking back at everything that happened, what is the most important lesson about being a bystander to cyberbullying?

A) Staying silent is always the safest option

B) It is better to wait and see if things resolve on their own

C) Bystanders have real power — choosing to act early can prevent harm from escalating ✅

D) Getting involved always makes things worse for everyone

Hint (Wrong Answer):
"Think about what would have happened at each stage if nobody had spoken up."

Correct Feedback:
"Correct. Research consistently shows that bystander intervention is one of the most effective ways to stop cyberbullying. Silence is never neutral — it signals that the behaviour is acceptable."
