CALL IT OUT

A cyberbullying awareness game

503IT · Communication & Collaboration · Coventry University London

You are Alex, 15. One evening you open your phone and discover your friend Jordan is being targeted online — across group chats, gaming lobbies, and social media.

Every decision you make has consequences. The right choices protect Jordan. The wrong ones let the situation spiral.

How to Play

📖

Read the scenario

Each stage puts you in a real social situation involving cyberbullying. Read every message carefully.

🎯

Make your choice

Pick how Alex responds. Four options — only one is the best action to take.

📊

Watch the meters

Trust = how supported Jordan feels. Situation = how far bullying has escalated. Keep Trust high, Situation low.

💡

Earn & spend points

Correct choices earn Awareness Points. Spend 5 points to reveal a hint.

⚠️

Don't let it escalate

If Situation hits 100% the stage restarts. Think carefully and act early.

🏆

Complete all 5 stages

Stages unlock in order. Finish all five to reach the Resolution and see your final score.

About This Game

What is CALL IT OUT?

CALL IT OUT is a browser-based, scenario-driven decision game designed to teach teenagers aged 12–17 how to recognise, respond to, and report cyberbullying.

The game places you in the role of a bystander — someone who witnesses cyberbullying happening to a friend. Research shows that bystander intervention is one of the most effective ways to stop cyberbullying.

The 5 Stages

  • 💬 The Group Chat — embarrassing content shared without consent
  • 🎮 The Gaming Lobby — exclusion and mockery in online gaming
  • 👤 The Fake Account — impersonation and identity theft
  • 🔥 The Pile On — pressure to participate and threats against bystanders
  • ✅ Resolution — reporting, supporting, and recovery
