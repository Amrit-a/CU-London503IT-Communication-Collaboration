# HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to CALL IT OUT.

Your friend is being cyberbullied.

Your mission is simple:

MAKE THE RIGHT CHOICES. BEFORE IT GETS WORSE.

---

Controls:

Navigate = Mouse click / Tap on screen

Select answer = Click your chosen option

Review feedback = Click Continue after each decision

---

Rules:

1. Read each scenario carefully before choosing your response

2. Select the response you think is most appropriate

3. Watch the Trust Meter and Situation Meter after every choice

4. If the Situation Meter fills up completely, the stage restarts

5. Earn Awareness Points for correct decisions

6. Spend Awareness Points to reveal hints on difficult questions

7. Complete all five stages to reach the Resolution

8. Your final score reflects how well you supported Jordan throughout

---

Meters Explained:

TRUST METER — tracks how much Jordan trusts you are helping

SITUATION METER — tracks how far the bullying has escalated

Keep the Trust Meter high. Keep the Situation Meter low.

---

Remember:

Think before you react.

Silence is not always safe.

Speaking up can change everything.

GOOD LUCK — Jordan is counting on you.
